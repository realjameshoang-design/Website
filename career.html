Career Goals

Gain experience in Cybersecurity

Never has fraud and data breaches been more common on the internet. Millions of people are in danger of losing sensitive information, potentially ruining their lives. I want to experience what working in cybersecurity is like because I want to help protect people. It seems like a career that can help others while also being interesting.

Learn about developing circuits

Our lives are full of electronics, and all of them have circuits. I want to learn more about the hardware aspect of computer engineering, because most of my focus so far has been with software.

Bring morals into technology

Many people disagree on how far we should take technology. How far is too far? How willing are we to sacrifice privacy and safety to make things easier? While I obviously don’t have a definitive answer to that, I want to maximize helping others while minimizing the negatives of going too far.

Make the lives of people easier

Convenience is key. We all have 24 hours in a day, and it’s annoying to waste some of those hours on mundane tasks. I want to save people time, so they can spend that time with family, friends, and hobbies.
